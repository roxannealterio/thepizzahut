The set menu

$60 per person

Food and a welcome drink, served share-style down the table: entrées like garlic focaccia, arancini, calamari and bruschetta, a selection of our red and white pizzas with pasta to share, salad and chips, then cannoli, tiramisù and our Dolce duo pizza.

Pizza, pasta & a garden

Our full menu is available for groups: red and white pizzas, homemade gnocchi and pasta, entrées, salads and desserts like tiramisù and our Dolce duo pizza. To drink there's Italian wine, cocktails like Aperol spritz and a classic negroni, and Brouhaha beer on the list.

We cater for vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free guests, with gluten-free bases and vegan cheese available on any pizza. There's a kids menu too, and the outdoor garden tables are dog friendly.

How it works

Booking a function

  1. Get in touch. Email or call with your date, time and how many guests you're expecting.
  2. Plan the food. We'll talk you through options from the menu to suit your group and any dietary needs.
  3. Enjoy the night. Turn up, sit back and let us look after the pizza, pasta and drinks.
Questions

Good to know

What kind of functions do you host?

Birthdays, meetings, work functions, Christmas parties, engagements, christenings and group dinners. If it involves good food and good company, get in touch.

How many people can you fit?

Our garden seats up to 25 guests. Tell us your group size and date and we'll take it from there.

How much is the function set menu?

$60 per person, including food served share-style and a welcome drink.

Can we bring decorations?

Yes, you're welcome to bring your own balloons and decorations.

Do you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes. We have vegetarian and vegan dishes, gluten-free pizza bases (+$6), vegan cheese (+$4) and gluten-free pasta options for the kids. Let us know about allergies when you enquire.

Can we bring the kids?

Absolutely. We have a kids menu for children 12 and under, with pizzas, nuggets and chips, and penne from $14. See our kids birthday parties page.

When are you open?

Wednesday and Thursday 5–8pm, Friday and Saturday 4–9pm, Sunday 4–8pm. Closed Monday and Tuesday.

Where are you?

Shop 4, 32 Asquith Street, Morningside QLD 4170, in Brisbane's east, close to Hawthorne, Balmoral, Cannon Hill and Norman Park.

How do I book?

Email hello@thepizzagarden.com.au or call 07 4800 5359. For regular table bookings you can also book online.

Let's plan it

Send us your date, rough number of guests and anything we should know (like dietary needs or allergies), and we'll get back to you.
